I'm a Data Analyst and researcher in Ho Chi Minh City, finishing a Bachelor of Financial Technology at the University of Economics and Law (VNU-HCM). My work sits between analytics engineering and forecasting research: I build pipelines and KPI layers that teams can trust, and I study how probabilistic models adapt when markets break.

I'm currently a **Data Analyst at TC-Data**, a data consulting firm, on the Solution Delivery team, where I work with clients to clarify requirements, define metrics and business logic, and turn them into data models and Power BI reporting. Previously at CASK, I built warehouse and reporting layers and automated recurring SQL, Power BI and Python workflows. On the research side, I'm first author of a [PAKDD 2026 workshop paper](/publications/) (Springer LNCS) on retrieval-guided Bayesian forecasting under structural breaks, and my team won **1st place at the Finnovative Hackathon 2026**.

In the near term I'm growing in data analytics and analytics engineering; my long-term goal is to become a Data Scientist, with graduate study in data science along the way.

**Core tools:** SQL · Python · Power BI · dbt · PostgreSQL · Dagster · Docker — full list on the [CV](/cv/).
